Who can enter

Students in PLSC 2219 are entered automatically through their course forecast.

Other Yale students are welcome to enter. Spots are limited, so sign up early. Everyone who signs up gets the course data and a guide to building a forecast. See the instructions.

Prizes

Prizes go to the most accurate forecasts once the results are in.

Want to show off your forecast? Post it on your own site with our template and we'll add it to the gallery, next to everyone else's.
