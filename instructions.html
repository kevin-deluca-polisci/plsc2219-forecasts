Instructions

How to Enter

These steps are for Yale students outside PLSC 2219. Students in the course follow the forecast assignment on Canvas and are entered automatically.

  1. Sign upSign up with a Yale email address and a forecaster nickname. Spots are limited.
  2. Get the dataEntrants receive a shared folder with the course data and an entrant guide covering the files and the forecast format.
  3. Build a forecastPredict the Democratic share of the two-party vote in each of the 470 House and Senate races.
  4. SubmitUpload forecast.csv through the submission form by Sunday, Nov. 1, 11:59 pm Eastern.
Sign up Submit your forecast

Submission

One file, forecast.csv, with a predicted vote share for every race. Win probabilities, ranges and simulations are optional.

The entrant guide covers the file format, races with special rules, and a script that checks the file before submission.

Forecast Sites (Optional)

The course website template turns a forecast into a public site like the class model's. Sites included with a submission are listed in the gallery under the entrant's nickname.

Rules

  • Open to Yale students. One entry per person; entries must be the entrant's own work.
  • Forecasts cover all 470 races.
  • The course data is for use in this competition only and may not be shared.
  • The last file submitted before the deadline counts.
  • Prizes go to the most accurate forecasts once the results are in.
  • Entries are listed by nickname. Email addresses are used only for contact and are not posted.

Hosted by the Democratic Innovations Program at ISPS, Yale University, with PLSC 2219. Questions? Contact Prof. Kevin DeLuca.
